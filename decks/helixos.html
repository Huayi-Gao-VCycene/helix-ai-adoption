Founders fail from confusion,
not lack of passion
First-time founders don't fail because they don't care. They fail because they don't have a system.

Broken Alternatives
Everything that exists
was built for someone else
Here's what founders are stuck with today:
Notion templates — static, generic, abandoned by week 2
YouTube rabbit holes — 10 tabs, no conclusions
Accelerator curricula — built for the average founder, not yours
Mentors — 1 hour/month, vague advice, no follow-through
AI chatbots — no venture context, starts from zero every session
The gap: founders need personalized, sequential, AI-generated guidance tied to their specific venture. That doesn't exist yet.
