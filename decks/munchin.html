The MVP
100
Videos before the full app build
The MVP is content, not code. One hundred videos, a waitlist, and feedback from people who actually try the meals. Then we build the app around proven behavior.
Publish repeatable TikTok formats — consistent look, sound, and pacing
Measure saves, comments, and reported meal attempts
Build the waitlist from top-performing recipes
Prototype only the features the audience actually asks for
