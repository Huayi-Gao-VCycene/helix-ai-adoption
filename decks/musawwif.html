02 · The Problem

Riba isn't a rate. It's a condition most Muslim businesses can't opt out of.

→
Every conventional loan, line of credit, and lease a business takes on is interest-based by default
→
Halal alternatives exist for buying a home — almost none exist for running a business
→
Owners who want out don't know where to start, or who to trust with the transition
→
Result: sincere business owners stay entangled in Riba for the life of their company — their income, their worship, and their Barakah compromised together
This isn't a lack of will. It's a lack of a pure path.
03 · Root Cause

The gap isn't awareness.
It's infrastructure.

→
Islamic finance was built for individuals, not businesses — halal mortgage and wealth platforms serve homebuyers; almost none serve an existing business restructuring its debt
→
Certification is a real barrier — Sharia-compliant contracts require legal, Sharia board, and securities expertise no founder assembles alone
→
Education is assumed, not delivered — owners are told to "go halal" with no curriculum on what that actually requires
→
Trust has to be earned one relationship at a time — nothing connects Masajid, business owners, and investors into a single system

05 · How It Works

From first conversation
to Pure Partnership.

Discovery

Education
Owner completes Musawwif's curriculum on Islamic financial ethics and what a Pure Partnership requires
Structuring
Musawwif matches the business with an equity partner and structures the Musharakah or Murabaha contract
Partnership
Interest-based debt is retired. Owner and investor share ownership, risk, and return going forward

07 · Business Model

We earn when the partnership works — not before.

Musawwif doesn't charge interest, and we don't charge upfront. Our revenue is a success fee, structured into the deal itself.

Investor-Side Fee

For some contracts, the success fee is built directly into the investor's return-sharing agreement once the partnership is structured.

Business-Side Success Fee

For others, the fee is paid by the business partner once the debt restructuring is complete and the Pure Partnership is live.

Which side pays depends on the contract and the problem being solved — but Musawwif only earns once Riba is actually replaced.
