One day more, together.
I know what it feels like
to struggle in silence.
My family and I learned together. We educated ourselves, supported one another, and discovered that healing isn't about finding one perfect solution — it's about taking small steps, having people who care, and believing that tomorrow can be better than today.
My name is Erica Alessandrini. One Day More grew out of my own mental health journey — and out of what I watched so many others go through when they reached out for help, only to find the system fragmented, expensive, and overwhelming.
The Problem
Help exists.
Finding it is the hard part.
Many youth experience mental health challenges but struggle to find timely, affordable, and connected support — and most wait until they're already in crisis.
Long Waitlists
Delays prevent youth from getting support when it's most needed — sometimes months on a waitlist
High Therapy Costs
Professional mental health services are not financially accessible for most young people
Nowhere to Begin
Many youth don't know where to start or which resources are trustworthy
Stigma & Silence
Fear of judgment stops young people from asking for help before they reach a crisis
The Gap
Existing platforms serve
one piece. Not the whole person.
Today, mental health support is spread across search engines, websites, crisis lines, social media, and counselling — all disconnected. Navigating them takes energy someone in distress doesn't have.
1 in 5
Canadians experience a mental health challenge — highest rates in youth ages 15–24
75%
of mental health problems begin before age 24 — early support changes outcomes
6+ mo.
Average wait time for publicly funded mental health services in Ontario
The gap isn't a lack of resources. It's a lack of connection between them.
The Solution
One platform.
The whole journey.
One Day More is a youth-centred digital mental health and wellness platform that brings trusted resources, wellness tools, and supportive connections together in one place — designed for early intervention, not crisis response.
Wellness Dashboard
Daily mood check-ins, journaling, goal tracking, affirmations — builds the habit of self-awareness before crisis
Support Circle
Invite trusted people — family, friends, caregivers — with full user control over what they see
Community Hub
Anonymous community space — you don't have to face this alone, even if you're not ready to name it
Resource Directory
Trusted local and national mental health resources — curated and easy to navigate, not a Google search
Key Feature
The Support Circle —
no other platform has this.
My family and I healed together. That experience showed me that the people around you matter — and that most platforms don't involve them at all.
How It Works
Users invite trusted people — a parent, a friend, a counsellor — into their Support Circle. They choose exactly what each person can see. No surprises. Full control. The user stays in charge of their story, while the people who care about them can actually help — not just worry from a distance.
User-controlled sharing
Optional collaborative safety plan
Trusted — not public
No existing platform offers this
