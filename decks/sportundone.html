My name is Alicia Johnson, founder of Sports Undone — the first platform designed to help women's sports fans travel, connect, and cheer together.
The Problem
Everything fans need
is everywhere but one place.
Today, fans rely on multiple disconnected platforms just to have one experience.
Sports websites for news and schedules
Social media for updates and reactions
Facebook groups or Reddit just to find other fans
Travel websites for flights and hotels
Result: fans often travel alone, miss community, and give up mid-planning
Women's sports are growing rapidly. The fan experience hasn't evolved with them.
The Solution
Sport Undone.
One platform. One fan journey.
Instead of planning across five websites, everything happens in one place.

Find games and events tailored to women's sports fans
Book Group Trips
Travel together, save together — logistics handled

Meet fans attending the same games before game day
Share & Belong
Build a supportive community — share memories, keep the energy alive
We're not just another travel platform. We're creating the home for women's sports fans.

Business Model
5 revenue streams.
Scalable as the community grows.
Membership Subscriptions
Recurring base revenue from fans who want full access
Group Travel Bookings
Commission on curated group trip packages — grows with volume
Ticket & Fan Experiences
Ticket sales and exclusive event access packages
Brand Sponsorships
Brands pay to reach an engaged, passionate women's sports audience
Affiliate Partnerships
Revenue share with hotels, airlines, and travel providers — every booking earns a cut
As the community grows, every revenue stream grows with it — creating a scalable and sustainable business.
Growth Strategy
Every trip creates content.
Every fan becomes an ambassador.
Social media content — showcase women's sports, fan stories, and travel experiences to build organic reach
Strategic partnerships — Porter Airlines, local hotels, sports teams, and event sponsors for co-marketing and bundled packages
On-the-ground activation — attend sports conferences, host fan meetups at major events to build trust and community firsthand
Every trip → content → referrals
Members who travel together become the word-of-mouth engine. No ad spend required to grow the next trip.
