The Hook
The fan experience
is still unfinished.
Imagine hearing about a women's sports game you'd love to attend. You want to go — and then the real work starts.
Where do I get tickets? No single trusted place for women's sports
Where do I stay? Travel planning is expensive and time-consuming
Is anyone else going? Many fans have no one to travel with
No platform focuses on women's sports travel end to end
The game is exciting. Everything around the game becomes friction.
The Problem
Women's sports fans
deserve better.
Fans already travel for sport. The planning and community layer around women's sports doesn't exist yet.
Pain points: cost, planning time, solo attendance, fragmented communities
Fans: women's sports fans, students, families, and sports travelers
10.5 million Canadian domestic trips in 2025 included attending a sports event as a spectator — Statistics Canada, June 2026. The behavior already exists. Sport Undone focuses it.
The Timing
Women's sports is moving
from niche to movement.
PWHL, WNBA, NWSL, and women's soccer are expanding audience attention fast
Fans want community around the event — not just a seat in the arena
Brands are actively looking for authentic women's sports communities to partner with
The travel layer for women's sports is still early. That gives Sport Undone a chance to become the trusted home for fans before a generic travel company tries to bolt this on later.

WNBA record viewership
